Academic Growth

Success in academics requires discipline, practice, and smart thinking. Below are key UNEB subjects and how you can improve in each one.

Mathematics

Scenario: You struggle with equations before exams.

Improve: Practice daily, solve past papers, and time yourself.

English

Scenario: Difficulty in comprehension and grammar.

Improve: Read novels, practice writing essays, learn new vocabulary.

Biology

Scenario: Hard to remember processes like digestion.

Improve: Use diagrams and teach others what you learn.

Chemistry

Scenario: Confused about formulas and reactions.

Improve: Memorize formulas and do experiments or examples.

Physics

Scenario: Difficulty understanding calculations.

Improve: Practice numerical questions and understand concepts.

Geography

Scenario: Forgetting physical features and locations.

Improve: Use maps and revise diagrams regularly.

History

Scenario: Too many dates and events.

Improve: Create timelines and summaries.

Computer Studies

Scenario: Lack of practical skills.

Improve: Practice typing, coding basics, and using software.

Boost Your IQ

- Solve puzzles and quizzes daily - Read books regularly - Practice memory techniques - Sleep well and stay healthy
